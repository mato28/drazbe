## How to add an item

1. Add a `// LEGAL-REVIEW: <short question>` comment at the relevant place in the code.
2. Add a row above: what's uncertain, the legal basis involved (e.g. ZFPPIPP article), where in code.
3. When resolved, record the decision and the source (lawyer opinion, court decision, article),
   update the code, and set the status to `RESOLVED`.
